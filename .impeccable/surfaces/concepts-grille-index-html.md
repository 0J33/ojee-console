# Surface: ojee.console prototype — The Grille

Scope: the whole console (Now, Home, Machines, Agent, Me, sheets, palette, idle clock) on a 1920×1080 desktop and an iPhone 13 Pro Max. Mode: operate. Sample data only.

## Direction contract
THESIS: The console is a piece of 1960s German hi-fi in black: one panel of keys, lamps and a perforated grille. The rut is a dark dashboard of cards; its opposite is a sci-fi cockpit HUD. Both are out, and so is rep.'s solid colour-tile look.
WORLD: AMOLED black body, graphite keys, cream lowercase legends, one colour per function (yellow is the one main key, green is running, red-orange is needs you). Archivo grotesk. No module colours.
FIRST VIEWPORT: one sentence of status with its lamp, the alarm clock with a yellow second hand, then one unit per module: object, number, grille.
SIGNATURE: every unit's grille is a live dot-matrix of its own history (signal, CPU, temperature, usage); scrub it to read a value.
OBJECTS: cream industrial objects on a black shelf, softly lit 3D: radio, hi-fi stack, calculator, turntable, thermostat dial, alarm clock.
RISK: low colour can bury state; lamps and words must carry it.
