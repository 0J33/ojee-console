The Grille

The console made like 1960s German hi-fi, in black. One panel of keys and lamps, a perforated grille that doubles as the display, and cream objects on a black shelf. Colour means a function, never a module.

rules

The off pixel

The body is #000, so an OLED switches it off. Depth comes from unit and key steps and from key relief.

Function colour

Yellow is the one main key and the second hand. Green is running. Red-orange is needs you. Modules have no colours.

Lamp and word

A state colour never stands alone: a lamp always sits beside a word.

One yellow key

At most one per screen, and it is the thing the screen is for.

Lowercase legends

Anything printed on the panel is lowercase. Content keeps its own case.

Glow budget

Only lamps and lit grille holes glow. Nothing else casts colour.
