Wireframe

Rules

True black

The ground is #000, so an OLED switches it off. Panels are barely lifted and separated by hairlines, not fills.

One accent

Lume cyan (#5FE6EC) marks the live second, the selected item, the main action and the latest reading. Nothing decorative is cyan.

State is a dot and a word

Green, amber and red only report state, and always sit beside the word that says it.

Hidden lines removed

Every 3D part is an invisible occluder plus its edges, so a wireframe reads as an object, not a diagram.

Real colours are content

The watch's lume and day wheel, a lit lamp, the keyboard's #00FFFF keys: shown as they are.

Mono means data

Geist for words; Geist Mono only for numbers, units, timestamps, scales and code.
